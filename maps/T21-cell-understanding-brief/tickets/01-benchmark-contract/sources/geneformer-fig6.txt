<div class="object margin-top-0-imp"><figure class="fig xbox font-sm" id="F6"><h1>Fig. 6 |. In silico treatment revealed candidate therapeutic targets.</h1>
<p class="img-box line-height-none margin-x-neg-2 tablet:margin-x-0 text-center"><img class="graphic" src="https://cdn.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/blobs/60a6/10949956/6386b0dd050a/nihms-1971443-f0006.jpg" loading="lazy" height="1703" width="1891" alt="Fig. 6 |"></p>
<figcaption><p id="P72"><strong>a,</strong> Fine-tuning Geneformer to distinguish cardiomyocytes from non-failing hearts or hearts affected by hypertrophic or dilated cardiomyopathy defines the embedding position of each cell state. Then, disease modeling <em>(left)</em> can be performed by in silico deleting or activating random genes within non-failing cardiomyocytes to define the random distribution (gray cloud) and thereby identify genes whose in silico deletion or activation shifts the embedding significantly towards either the hypertrophic or dilated cardiomyopathy state. The reverse approach is taken for in silico treatment analysis <em>(center and right)</em>. <strong>b,</strong> Out-of-sample predictions of Geneformer fine-tuned to distinguish cardiomyocytes from non-failing hearts or hearts affected by hypertrophic or dilated cardiomyopathy. Accuracy: 90%, precision: 82%, recall 87%. (Training data: non-failing n=9, hypertrophic n=11, dilated n=9, total 93,589 cells; out-of-sample data: non-failing n=4, hypertrophic n=4, dilated n=2, total 39,006 cells). <strong>c,</strong> Hierarchical clustering of fine-tuned Geneformer cardiomyocyte cell embeddings. <strong>d,</strong> Overlap of genes whose in silico deletion in cardiomyocytes from non-failing hearts significantly shifted the fine-tuned Geneformer cell embeddings towards the hypertrophic or dilated cardiomyopathy states and Gene Ontology terms enriched for each state. <strong>e,</strong> Distribution of mean embedding shift in response to in silico deletion of candidate therapeutic targets in cardiomyocytes from hypertrophic cardiomyopathy (n=104 genes). <strong>f,</strong> Contractile force of cardiac microtissues derived from <em>WT</em> iPSCs or iPSCs with a <em>TTN</em> truncating mutation modeling dilated cardiomyopathy (<em>WT</em> n=11, <em>TTN</em><sup>+/−</sup> n=12, *p&lt;0.05 Wilcoxon). <strong>g,</strong> Contractile stress (force per unit area) of cardiac microtissues derived from <em>TTN</em><sup>+/−</sup> iPSCs exposed to either control treatment or guides promoting CRISPR-mediated knockout of Geneformer-predicted therapeutic targets. (<em>TTN</em><sup>+/−</sup> +control treatment n=22, <em>TTN</em><sup>+/−</sup> +CRISPR guides targeting knockout of <em>PLN</em> n=22, <em>GSN</em> n=7, <em>ESRRG</em> n=9, or <em>HMGB1</em> n=11; p&lt;0.05 Wilcoxon, FDR-corrected). In (f-g): center line=median, box limits=upper and lower quartiles, whiskers=1.5x interquartile range, points=experimental replicates.</p></figcaption></figure></div></article>
